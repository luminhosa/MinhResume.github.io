About the project

The internal profile series introduces the people clients may work with directly. Each video focuses on an individual's story, beliefs, and aspirations for the clients they serve.

I conducted pre-recording interviews with the featured people and used those conversations to craft scripts that bring out their stories. I then oversaw filming and editing so each profile remained clear and personal.

The visual approach supports personal branding. I helped each person appear professional while still looking natural and approachable, and designed a set that felt both warm and credible to potential clients.
