About the project

A four-season podcast that helps listeners gain clarity in life decisions and make wise financial choices. It began as audio-only in 2021 and later expanded into video.

I developed questions on trending topics, managed studio sound, edited audio, and designed podcast graphics and short-form videos. In 2024 I set up the upgraded studio for improved sound and video recording, and selected clips from longer episodes for reels.
