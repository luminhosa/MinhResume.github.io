About the project

For this digital imaging project, I imagined what I might create as an editor for Game of Thrones. The result is a fictional screen designed to fit a fantastical world rather than a real frame from the show.

I added a floating island and warships, then edited the original background to give the whole image a softer, more ethereal feel. The gallery includes the before and after images so the changes can be seen directly.
