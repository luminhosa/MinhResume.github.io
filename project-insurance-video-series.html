About the project

This series is designed for working adults aged 25 to 35 who may not be financially literate. It explains essential insurance topics in an engaging and accessible way, helping viewers make better informed decisions.

I was responsible for the production from beginning to end. For each episode, I brainstormed the key discussion points, filmed the conversation, and oversaw the editing process.

Because the series was new for a relatively young company, I followed its brand guidelines closely. The aim was to keep the presentation consistent while balancing a fun, approachable tone with the professionalism expected of financial content.
