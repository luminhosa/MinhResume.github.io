About the project

The four-season podcast helps its audience gain clarity about life decisions and make wise financial choices. It began in 2021 as an audio-only podcast and developed into an audio and video production.

I crafted questions around trending topics, made sure the studio sound was high quality, and edited audio into a polished listening experience. I also designed the graphics for the podcast and its related short-form videos.

In 2024, the recording setup was upgraded to improve sound and enable video episodes. I set up the studio and managed the equipment. From the longer videos, I selected interesting moments for reels to increase exposure across platforms; the presentation also shows how the episode thumbnails evolved across all four seasons.
