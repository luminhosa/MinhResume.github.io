About the project

Guardians of the Lonely Departed is a short film about Tommy Yu and the care he gives to elders at the end of their lives. He performs last rites for people who have no one else to do so.

The film brings attention to a quiet act of service that can otherwise go unseen. It was recognised as MTD301 Film of the Year at the Singapore University of Social Sciences in 2023.
